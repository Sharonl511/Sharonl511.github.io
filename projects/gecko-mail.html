Project Notes

01Performance

Rory is a gecko mail carrier in a fictional, Nordic-inspired world. On a cold day, he opens a metal mailbox with his tongue to deliver the mail, and his tongue freezes to the metal. The rest of this 20-second clip is his struggle to pull himself free. My goal was to show that I can animate a character pushing and pulling against objects so that Rory and everything he touches feel like they have real weight. Every tug has to read as believable force, so the audience feels the strain along with him.

02Process

The biggest challenge was making the stuck-tongue performance believable. There was no way to film real reference of a tongue frozen to metal, so I couldn't simply act it out and copy it. Instead, I gathered other reference footage to build from, and I used a stretchy elastic cord to simulate the pull. Working with the cord let me observe how that pull really feels, and I used what I learned to guide Rory's struggle against the mailbox.
