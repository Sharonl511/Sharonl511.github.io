Project Notes

01Performance

White is a teenager auditioning for her school's theater production. As the clip opens, she is fully in character as a lady telling the story of her husband's death and how much she hates him. Then a teacher interrupts, and the role drops away to reveal the real girl underneath. My goal in this 20-second lip-sync piece was to make that sudden change read clearly through my acting choices, so the audience gets the twist the instant it happens. The comedy depends on the contrast between the dramatic role she is playing and her real self.

02Process

The biggest challenge was switching between two worlds: the scene in White's imagination, where she is the lady, and the real school audition. To make each switch clear, I changed the lighting, camera movement, and scene transitions between the two settings, and I swapped her costume model so her look changes along with the scene. Another major challenge was her hair and skirt. I groomed her hair with XGen and simulated her skirt with nCloth.

03Breakdown

Everything from character design to final edit · ZBrush, Maya (AdvancedSkeleton, XGen, nCloth), Substance Painter, Arnold, Nuke, Photoshop, Premiere
