I'm Yixuan Lu, a character performance animator finishing my BFA in Computer Animation at Ringling College of Art + Design, class of 2027. My work is aimed at games, film, and TV, with a focus on storytelling: clear A-to-B poses, lip-sync, and characters that interact convincingly with props and environments.

I take each project through the full 3D pipeline myself. I design the character, model it in ZBrush and Maya, texture it in Substance Painter, and build full-body, face, and prop rigs in Maya, with blendshapes that range from casual to extreme. Then I animate, render in Arnold, composite in Nuke, and edit in Premiere. My full-body shots have ranged from an adult man to a teenage girl to a gecko creature, each refined through rounds of critique. I also animate in 2D with Photoshop and Animation Paper.

My ideas usually start with everyday life, and I like seeing different perspectives combine into something new. I'm currently making my thesis film with one teammate, and we work through problems together at every stage of production. In 2024 I interned as an editor in Shanghai, cutting more than 15 short promotional videos a day in CapCut with a marketing team.

I keep my files organized so teammates can pick up my work easily, and I work in both English and Mandarin. I'd love to hear what your team is working on.
